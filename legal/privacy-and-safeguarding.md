## 1. The short version (for students)

- EduQuest helps you practise for school and exams. To do that we need a few details about you, like your name, class and state.
- Other students only ever see your **first name and the first letter of your surname**, your state, and your scores. Never your phone number, email, address or photo.
- There is **no chat** between students. Nobody can send you a message through EduQuest.
- If you are **under 18**, a parent or guardian has to agree before you can enter competitions that have prizes and before you can claim a prize. Practising and learning do not need this.
- We do not sell your information and we do not show you adverts.
- You (or your parent or guardian) can ask us to show you your information or delete your account at any time.
- If something worries you, tell a parent, a teacher or us at **[PLACEHOLDER: safeguarding contact email and phone]**.

## 3. Who can use EduQuest

EduQuest is made for students in Nigerian secondary schools (JSS1 to SS3) and people preparing for exams such as WAEC, NECO and JAMB. Many of our students are **children**. In this policy a child is anyone **under 18**, as in the Child's Rights Act 2003.

## 4. What we collect, why, and who sees it

| What | Why we need it | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email, password (stored scrambled, never readable), username | To make and secure your account | You and EduQuest staff who need it |
| Phone number (optional) | To reach you about prizes and your account | You and EduQuest staff. Never other students |
| Class, state, age, school (optional) | To show the right questions, to check you can enter a competition, and to place you on state and zone leaderboards | You and staff. Others see your **state** only |
| What you answer, your scores, XP, coins, streaks, badges, certificates | To run the learning, rewards and competitions | You. Others see scores and rank on leaderboards under your first name and surname initial |
| Questions you ask the AI tutor | To answer you | You and the systems that run the tutor. See section 7 |
| Your voice, only if you speak to the tutor | To turn what you say into text. This is done on our own server and the recording is deleted straight afterwards | Nobody. The text is treated like any other tutor question |
| Profile photo, only if you sign in with Google | It comes with your Google account | You only. We do not show it to anyone else |
| Competition entries, results, prize claims | To run fair competitions and deliver prizes | You and staff. Winners' first name, surname initial and state may be shown on results |
| Parent or guardian name and phone number | To record their agreement and to deliver prizes safely | Staff who handle consent and prizes only |
| Device notification token | To send reminders you have not switched off | Our notification provider |
| Basic technical logs (time, request, approximate error information) | To keep the service secure and fix faults | Staff who run the service |

We collect only what we need. We do **not** collect your precise location, your contacts, or photos and files from your phone, and we do not ask for government ID numbers.

## 5. What we will not do

- We will **not sell** personal information, and we will not share it with advertisers.
- We will **not** show targeted adverts to students.
- We will **not** build a public profile of any student, and we do not show profile photos, usernames or full names to other students anywhere: not on leaderboards, competition pages or when students search for someone to challenge.
- We will **not** let students message or contact each other. Challenges between students are quiz matches only, with no free-text communication.
- We will **not** give sponsors any personal information about students. Sponsors appear only as a logo on competition pages. A sponsor who wants to reach a winner has to go through us.
- We will **not** use a child's photo, voice, name or story in marketing without the written agreement of a parent or guardian.

## 6. Why we are allowed to use your information

- To give you the service you signed up for (your account, practice, scores and competitions).
- Because a parent or guardian has agreed, where the law or this policy requires it (section 8).
- Because we have a good reason that does not override your rights, such as keeping the service secure and preventing cheating in competitions.
- Because the law requires it.

## 7A. Competitions, prizes and certificates

- A competition page may show a winner's **first name, surname initial, state and score**.
- Prizes are delivered to the contact details given when claiming. Devices and scholarships also need a parent or guardian's phone number.
- Certificates carry the student's full name on the PDF that only they can download. Anyone who has a certificate's verification code can check it is real and will see **only the first name and surname initial**.

## 8. Children: parent and guardian agreement

**Why.** Children need extra protection, and prizes involve contact, delivery and sometimes money.

**What needs agreement.** If you are under 18, or we do not know your age, a parent or guardian must agree **before you can enter a competition that has prizes, and before you can claim a prize that has to be delivered**. Learning, practice, the AI tutor and competitions without prizes do not need it. **[PLACEHOLDER: counsel to confirm that this approach satisfies the Nigeria Data Protection Act's requirements for children's data, or whether agreement is needed before an account can be created at all. If so, change this section and the app before launch.]**

**How it works.**
1. The student (or their parent) enters the parent or guardian's name and phone number and confirms the parent or guardian agrees to this policy.
2. We record the name, number, the date and the version of this policy that was agreed.
3. We may call or message the number to confirm before releasing a prize.

**Taking agreement back.** A parent or guardian can ask us at any time to remove their agreement. We will do so, the student will no longer be able to enter prize competitions, and we will tell them why. Contact **[PLACEHOLDER]**.

**Limits.** We cannot be certain who is on the other end of a phone number. If we have reason to doubt the agreement is genuine, we will hold the prize and check.

2. **No public personal details.** Others see first name, surname initial, state and scores only.
3. **Staff access is limited.** Only named administrators can see student details. ## 11. Your rights

You, or a parent or guardian for a child, can ask us to:

- **See** the information we hold (signed-in students can download their own data from the app).
- **Correct** anything wrong.
- **Delete** the account and its information.
- **Stop** using it for something that is not needed to run the service.
- **Take a copy** to move elsewhere.
- **Complain** to us, and to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Write to **[PLACEHOLDER: email]**. We will respond within **[PLACEHOLDER: for example 30 days]** and may ask for proof that the request comes from the student or their parent or guardian before acting. Deleting an account is permanent.

## 12. Security

Passwords are stored in a scrambled form that we cannot read. Traffic to our servers is encrypted. Staff access to personal details is restricted by role and recorded. We cannot promise that nothing will ever go wrong, which is why section 9 says what we do if it does.

## 13. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes in a way that matters we will change the version number, tell users in the app, and ask them (and parents or guardians where agreement was needed) to agree again. The version a person agreed to is stored with their account.
